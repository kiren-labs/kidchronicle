Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Applies to KidChronicle web app

Short version: KidChronicle stores all your data on your device only. We have no servers, no accounts, and no access to any information you enter.

What data we collect

KidChronicle collects nothing. There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no network requests to any server operated by KidChronicle.

The only network requests the app makes are to load its own files (HTML, CSS, JS, icon fonts) on first launch. After that it works entirely offline.

Where your data is stored

All information you enter — family name, child profiles, log entries, points, and reflections — is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB on your device. It never leaves your device unless you use the Export feature to create a backup file yourself.

Export and import

The Export feature creates a JSON file that is downloaded directly to your device. KidChronicle never receives or sees this file. If you use Import, the file is read locally in your browser — it is not uploaded anywhere.

Children's privacy

KidChronicle is designed for parents to record notes about their own children. Because no data is transmitted to any server, the app does not collect any personal information about children, and COPPA and similar regulations that govern online collection of children's data do not apply.

Third-party services

KidChronicle uses the following third-party resources, all of which are self-hosted in the app itself after first load:

  • Tabler Icons — MIT-licensed icon font, served from this app's own files
  • Google Fonts (Fraunces, DM Sans) — loaded from Google's servers on first visit if not cached. See Google's privacy policy.

Google Fonts is the only third-party service that may see your IP address (as part of the font CSS request on first load). No personal information from within the app is involved.

Data deletion

To delete all your data, go to Settings → Reset all data in the app. This clears all entries from your device immediately and cannot be undone. You can also clear app data through your browser or device settings.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Since we have no way to contact users (no accounts, no email), we encourage you to review this page occasionally.

Contact
